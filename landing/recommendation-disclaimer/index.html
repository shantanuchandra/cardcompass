Recommendation disclaimer

CardCompass makes assumptions visible so you can inspect a card choice. An estimate is still not an issuer decision, a guarantee or personalised professional advice.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

CardCompass outputs are informational. They are not financial advice, investment advice, legal advice or tax advice and are not an instruction to apply for credit, spend more, carry a balance or complete a transaction.

Outputs depend on inputs

A comparison is only as reliable as the card rules, transaction details, statement records, merchant/category assumptions, reward valuation and cap/milestone progress available to it. Public calculators use only values you enter and deliberately omit many issuer rules. Signed-in features can use more context but can still be incomplete or stale.

Issuer and merchant terms control

The card issuer, network, merchant or booking platform decides eligibility, posting, reward credit, reversals and benefit inventory. Official issuer terms and the actual checkout disclosure are authoritative. CardCompass may link to or cite a source, but links can change and a source can be superseded.

Conditions that can change a result

  • Merchant category code, payment channel, wallet or aggregator routing.
  • Minimum transaction value, excluded categories and eligible card variants.
  • Monthly, quarterly, annual, per-transaction or per-customer caps.
  • Statement-cycle dates, posting dates, refunds, reversals and supplementary-card spend.
  • Reward-point redemption value, transfer ratios and expiry.
  • Milestone eligibility, enrolment requirements and whether a purchase is sensible on its own.
  • Movie inventory, day/showtime, ticket count, network, quota, remaining use and convenience fees.

Verify before acting

Before payment, verify the issuer's current terms, merchant/offer page and the card selected at checkout. After payment, check the posted transaction and reward statement. If a recommendation conflicts with an issuer's current material, rely on the issuer and report the mismatch to support@cardcompass.in.

Do not spend for an estimate alone

A projected reward or milestone value may be lower than the cost of an unnecessary purchase, interest, late fees or a missed payment. Pay bills according to the issuer agreement. CardCompass does not assess affordability, creditworthiness or whether taking credit is appropriate for you.

Illustrations and labels

Examples labelled illustrative, demo or calculator output are not live recommendations. A “winner” means the largest estimated value under the displayed inputs, not the universally best card. “On track” means the entered planned spend reaches the arithmetic target; it does not prove that all spend will count.

No guarantee of accuracy or availability

We work to show sources and caveats, but we do not guarantee completeness, timeliness, accuracy, reward posting or uninterrupted availability. Recommendations can be corrected or withdrawn as sources and product logic change.

Questions and corrections

Send a source link, card name, relevant date and a description of the discrepancy to support@cardcompass.in. Do not include a full card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or banking password.
