How this estimate works

For BOGO, eligible pairs are the smaller of complete ticket pairs and whole remaining redemptions; each eligible pair creates raw savings equal to one entered ticket price. Percentage and fixed offers apply once to the purchase whenever at least one redemption remains; extra remaining uses do not multiply that purchase's discount. The calculator then applies the savings cap and never discounts more than the ticket subtotal. Entered convenience fees are added after ticket savings.

BOGO eligible pairs = min(floor(ticket count ÷ 2), remaining uses)
BOGO raw saving = eligible pairs × ticket price
percentage raw saving = ticket subtotal × offer % (once when remaining uses ≥ 1)
estimated saving = smaller of raw saving, cap and ticket subtotal

BOGO rarely means every charge is free

The free ticket is often capped, limited to one pair and tied to equal or lower-priced seats. Taxes, food and convenience fees can remain payable.

Quota is part of eligibility

Some offers have daily or monthly inventory. A card can meet the written rule while the booking platform reports that the allocation is exhausted.

What this does not include

The calculator does not contact BookMyShow, PVR INOX, an issuer or a card network. It cannot confirm a supported card variant, platform, network, city, cinema, showtime, seat category, minimum booking value, promo code, inventory, issuer quota, remaining use, payment channel or validity date. It does not handle mixed ticket prices or a rule that discounts only the cheaper ticket.

Taxes and convenience fees vary by booking. Enter a realistic per-ticket fee, then verify the final checkout screen. A failed or reversed transaction can consume or later restore quota differently across programmes.

Worked BOGO example

Two tickets cost ₹450 each and the offer says buy one get one with maximum savings of ₹300. Raw BOGO savings would be ₹450, but the cap reduces the estimate to ₹300. At ₹35 convenience fee per ticket, total charges before the offer are ₹970 and the estimated payable amount is ₹670. Effective savings on the fee-inclusive total are about 30.93%, not 50%.

This arithmetic still assumes the offer is available, the card and show qualify, one use remains and both tickets use the entered price.

Compare offers on the final payable amount

A “50% off” headline and a BOGO headline can produce different results once each cap is applied. Run each offer separately with its own cap and remaining use. Compare estimated payable, not only percentage. Also consider whether another card's base rewards apply when a promotional discount does not.

Checkout verification checklist

  1. Open the issuer's current offer terms and the booking platform's offer page.
  2. Confirm card variant and network; Visa, Mastercard, RuPay and American Express versions can differ.
  3. Check the offer day, sale window, showtime and cinema eligibility.
  4. Check ticket minimum/maximum, free-ticket limit and maximum savings.
  5. Confirm remaining monthly/quarterly uses and whether quota is first-come-first-served.
  6. Apply the required offer route or promo code and inspect the final payable amount before authorising.
  7. Keep the checkout confirmation if the issuer or platform needs it for a dispute.

Sources and methodology

The calculator applies the formulas above to the offer value, whole-number remaining uses and fees you enter; it does not check live inventory. Read the CardCompass recommendation methodology and disclaimer, the booking platform's current offer terms, and the Reserve Bank of India credit- and debit-card FAQ. Issuer and merchant terms remain authoritative for eligibility and fulfilment.

Frequently asked questions

Does BOGO mean 50% off?

Only in a simple equal-price pair with no cap or extra fees. A cap and convenience fees can make effective savings much lower.

What should I enter for a 25% offer?

Select percentage, enter 25 as the offer value, and enter the maximum rupee discount from the terms.

Can the calculator tell whether quota remains?

No. Enter zero remaining uses if you know the allowance is exhausted, but verify real-time inventory and eligibility at checkout.
