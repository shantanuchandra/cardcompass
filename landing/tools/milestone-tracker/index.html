How this estimate works

The tracker subtracts eligible spend so far from the target. It then adds spend you already plan and shows any projected gap. Projected-gap daily pace is that gap divided by days left; it is a neutral planning number, not a recommendation to spend that amount every day.

remaining now = max(target − eligible spend, 0)
projected gap = max(target − eligible spend − planned spend, 0)
projected-gap daily pace = projected gap ÷ days remaining

“On track” is narrow

The label means only that your entered current plus planned spend reaches the threshold. It does not confirm that the issuer will count each transaction or award the milestone.

A target is not free money

If you need ₹20,000 of unnecessary spend to earn a ₹2,000 voucher, the arithmetic is not attractive. Interest or late fees can overwhelm the value even faster.

What this does not include

The calculator does not connect to an issuer. It cannot know pending versus posted transactions, refunds, reversals, supplementary-card rules, category exclusions, EMI treatment, wallet loads, rent, government payments, insurance, fuel, fees, taxes or the issuer's exact cycle boundary. It does not value the milestone reward or compare that value with another card's lost rewards.

It also cannot decide whether the planned purchase is affordable or useful. Treat the result as a reconciliation aid and verify progress in the issuer app or statement.

Find the correct target period first

Milestones can run by calendar month, calendar quarter, membership year, card anniversary or statement cycle. A “quarter” in marketing copy may not match the three months you expect. Record the start and end dates from the issuer terms. When the final day is close, remember that authorisation date and posting date can differ.

Which spend might not count?

Every programme is different, but common exclusions include cash advances, balance transfers, fees and interest, refunds, rent, wallet loading, fuel, government payments, education, utilities, insurance, tax payments and EMI conversion. Some milestones count net retail purchases only. Some exclude transactions that later become EMI. Use the issuer's current wording rather than this general list.

Worked example

A card has a ₹1,00,000 quarterly milestone. The issuer shows ₹72,000 of eligible posted spend and the period ends in 14 days. You already plan ₹8,000 of normal purchases. The remaining amount now is ₹28,000; current plus planned spend is ₹80,000; the projected gap is ₹20,000; and the projected-gap daily pace is ₹1,428.57.

If the milestone benefit is worth ₹1,500, manufacturing ₹20,000 of purchases is not justified. If ₹20,000 of unavoidable eligible bills are already scheduled, the milestone may be relevant—but those bills still need to post in time and qualify.

A safer milestone routine

  1. Copy the target, benefit, eligible-spend definition and exact period from the issuer.
  2. Use posted eligible spend, not the card's total outstanding balance.
  3. Subtract refunds and known exclusions.
  4. List only purchases you already intended and can pay in full.
  5. Compare the benefit value with rewards you give up by moving purchases from another card.
  6. Leave a posting-time buffer and verify after the cycle ends.

Sources and methodology

The tracker calculates only from the figures you enter; it does not verify eligible spend. Read the CardCompass recommendation methodology and disclaimer, consult the issuer's current milestone terms, and compare them with the Reserve Bank of India credit- and debit-card FAQ. Issuer rules remain authoritative for exclusions, posting windows and fulfilment.

Frequently asked questions

Does all card spend count?

No. Use the issuer's eligible-spend definition. Outstanding balance is not the same as milestone progress.

What if there are zero days left?

The calculator shows that no daily pace can be computed. Check posted progress with the issuer and do not assume a same-day purchase will count.

Does “reached” guarantee the benefit?

No. It means the number you entered meets the numeric target. Eligibility, posting and reward fulfilment remain with the issuer.
