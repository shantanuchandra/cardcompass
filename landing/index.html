    Before you apply

    A few useful details.

    Who is Founding 100 early access for?

    We are prioritising people who actively use multiple Indian credit cards and regularly compare rewards, benefits, or milestones. Applying does not guarantee selection.

    Why do you ask about my cards and spend range?

    Those answers help us understand whether the early-access experience fits your needs. You can name up to two cards, but that field is optional.

    Will I receive marketing email?

    Only if you select the separate product-updates checkbox. We may still email about your waitlist application or invitation.

    Is the interactive receipt a real recommendation?

    No. It is explicitly illustrative and demonstrates how CardCompass intends to present a decision. It does not use your cards or live issuer rules.
