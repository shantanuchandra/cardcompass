Common Questions

Is my Gmail data safe?

CardCompass uses read-only OAuth — we can only read emails, never send or delete. We scan only for bank statement emails. No email content is stored on our servers.

What data does CardCompass store?

We store your card configurations and transaction categories — not raw transaction data or statement PDFs. Everything is processed in-memory and discarded after extraction.

Which banks are supported?

HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, RBL, IndusInd, IDFC First, Amex, Citi, Standard Chartered, Yes Bank, AU Small Finance, Federal Bank, and Bank of Baroda — with more added regularly.

Does this work with encrypted PDF statements?

Yes. CardCompass auto-guesses statement passwords using your birthday and other common formats that Indian banks use.

Is CardCompass free?

Early access is completely free. We'll introduce premium features later — early access users will get a special rate.
